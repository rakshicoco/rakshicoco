==================================================
1. CRITICAL BUSINESS CLARIFICATION
==================================================

THE BUSINESS DOES NOT PURCHASE FARMS, LAND, GARDENS, OR LOTS.

The business purchases COCONUTS from different farms/gardens.

Therefore:

FARM = permanent master entity/source
PURCHASE = actual coconut procurement transaction
PURCHASE BATCH = one particular coconut purchase

Do NOT create a "land purchase" concept.

Do NOT model "Lot" as land ownership.

The PDF uses the term "Lot" for traceability, but in this implementation replace that concept with:

Purchase
or
Purchase Batch

Use a unique Purchase ID such as:

PUR-2026-0001

Every purchase must remain traceable through:

→ GROUPING

4. 40-DAY HARVEST CYCLE / FARM REMINDERS
==================================================

THIS IS A CRITICAL CLIENT REQUIREMENT.

After a harvest is completed, the next expected harvest is approximately 40 days later.

Business rule:

next_expected_harvest_date =
actual_harvest_date + 40 days

Example:

Harvest completed:
17 September 2026

Next expected harvest:
27 October 2026

The system must automatically create a follow-up/reminder.

The owner needs to be reminded to approach/contact the farmer before the next expected harvest.

Do NOT assume the harvest is guaranteed exactly after 40 days.

Call it:

"Expected Harvest"
or
"Farm Follow-up Date"

not a guaranteed harvest date.

Reminder system should support:

- upcoming
- due today
- overdue
- contacted
- negotiating
- purchased
- postponed
- skipped

The owner should be able to manually change the follow-up date if needed.

Recommended reminders:

- Early reminder
- Due reminder
- Overdue reminder

These should be configurable in Settings.

Example:

35 days after harvest → early reminder
38 days → reminder
40 days → due
42+ days → overdue

Do not hard-code these reminder offsets if they can reasonably be configurable.

Dashboard must prominently show:

FARM FOLLOW-UPS

TODAY
Ravi Farm
Expected harvest: Today
[Approach Farmer]

UPCOMING
Kumar Farm — 2 days
Muthu Farm — 5 days
Raja Farm — 7 days

OVERDUE
Farm X — 3 days overdue

The owner must be able to mark:

[Contacted]
[Negotiating]
[Purchased]
[Postponed]
[Skipped]

When a new purchase is created for the farm, the previous reminder should automatically be resolved and a new 40-day cycle should begin after the actual harvest completion.

==================================================
