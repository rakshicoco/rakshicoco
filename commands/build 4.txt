Continue the Rakshi Coco ERP implementation.

PHASE 1: AUTHENTICATION & SETTINGS

Do NOT move to Phase 2 until every Phase 1 requirement below is implemented, verified, and the application builds successfully.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES:
- This is a coconut procurement/processing/sales ERP.
- Farms are permanent master records.
- The business purchases coconuts from farms; it does NOT purchase land/farms.
- Purchase/Purchase Batch is the transaction.
- Farm → many Purchases.
- Actual harvest completion starts the approximate +40-day next-harvest cycle.
- Use Supabase Auth as the authentication source.
- Never create a duplicate authentication system.
- Never expose SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY to the browser/client.
- Never hard-code secrets.
