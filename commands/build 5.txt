============================================================
PRODUCT
============================================================

Product name:

RAKSHI COCO ERP

Purpose:

A complete ERP for a coconut procurement, harvesting, processing, stock, sales, transport, labour, billing, payments and profitability business.

The real business workflow is:

FARM
 ↓
PURCHASE
 ↓
HARVEST / CUTTING
 ↓
GROUPING
 ↓
FARM → GODOWN TRANSPORT
 ↓
GODOWN
 ↓
PROCESSING / DEHUSKING
 ↓
READY STOCK
 ↓
BUYER SALE
 ↓
GODOWN → BUYER TRANSPORT
 ↓
DISPATCH
 ↓
DELIVERY
 ↓
BILL
 ↓
PAYMENT
 ↓
RECEIVABLE / PAYABLE
 ↓
PROFIT & LOSS
 ↓
CASH FLOW

IMPORTANT:

The business purchases COCONUTS FROM FARMS.

The business does NOT purchase land, farms or agricultural lots.

A Farm is a permanent master entity.

A Purchase/Purchase Batch is a transaction.

Example:

FARM-0001
  ├── PUR-0001
  ├── PUR-0002
  ├── PUR-0003
  └── PUR-0004

Never model a Farm as something purchased.
